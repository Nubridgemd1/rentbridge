The flow

From request to repayment

Six stages, most of them instant. Money moves straight to the landlord, and repayment is automatic from a card on file.

STAGE 1

Request

The renter states the rent owed, the amount needed (up to one month), the due date, and the landlord. RentBridge shows the maximum advance instantly.

STAGE 2

Verify

Identity (KYC + selfie), bank connection for income and cash-flow, and optional rent-payment history. Read-only access - no funds can be pulled outside the authorized repayments.

STAGE 3

Decision

A transparent risk score combines income, rent-to-income, verification depth and rent history into an instant approved amount, fee and repayment plan aligned to the renter's payday.

STAGE 4

Card on file & mandate

Before funding, the renter puts a card on file and signs the autopay authorization (optionally adding ACH as backup). No card, no loan.

STAGE 5

Disburse to landlord

Funds are sent directly to the landlord for the rent - never to the renter's spending account - so the money can only do the job it was borrowed for.

STAGE 6

Automatic repayment

The card on file is charged on each scheduled date. On-time payments are reported to the bureaus; a missed charge triggers auto-retry, the backup method, and an early-warning/hardship path. Loan closes when repaid.

Timing: a complete request is typically approved in under two minutes, with rent disbursed to the landlord the same day - fast enough to beat a rent deadline.
